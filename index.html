02 / The journey

Every car
has a story.

  1. 01

    The problem

    It starts small. A pull to the left. A shake at 80. A tyre that won't hold air. A light on the dashboard that won't go away.

    Backdrop: real photo, engine bay at Bajrang Motors
  2. 02

    Inspection

    Before anything is fixed, it's checked. Wheel angles, tread, suspension, battery. Looking for the cause, not just the symptom.

    Backdrop: real photo, the 3D alignment rig
  3. 03

    Repair

    On the machines along the blue wall: the alignment rig, the wheel balancer, the tyre changer. Then the hands-on work in the bays.

    Backdrop: real photo, balancer & tyre changers
  4. 04

    Ready

    Level. Balanced. Aligned. The car that came in limping goes out standing straight.

  5. 05

    Back on the road

    Straight steering, no shake, nothing on the dashboard. Just the road ahead.
